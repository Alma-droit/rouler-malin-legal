Privacy Policy

Rouler Malin app for iPhone. Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: Rouler Malin works without an account and without a server. Your vehicles, maintenance records, invoices and photos are stored only on your iPhone. The publisher of the app has no access to them.

1. Data controller

Adam Joucla, sole proprietor (Thalyc Studio, SIREN 132 002 221, France), contact: adam@almaplace.fr.

2. Data stored on your phone

The information you enter (vehicles, mileage, maintenance, expenses, reminders, display name), as well as invoice photos and PDFs and the photo of your vehicle, is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the publisher or to any third party.

This data is included in your device's iCloud backup if you have turned it on; that backup is managed by Apple in your own account. It only leaves the app if you share it yourself (PDF export, backup file).

3. Purchases and subscription

Purchases are processed by Apple. To check your Premium access, the app uses the RevenueCat service (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app and your purchase history. No vehicle data and no invoice is sent to it. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (providing the subscription). This data is kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to manage purchases and meet legal obligations.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take a photo of an invoice or of your vehicle.
  • Photo library and files: only to import a photo or a PDF that you choose.
  • Notifications: deadline reminders scheduled on the phone itself; no data is sent.

5. No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tool and no tracker, and does not track you within the meaning of Apple's App Tracking Transparency.

6. Your rights

You can erase all your data at any time from Settings › My profile › "Erase all my data", or by deleting the app. For the data held by RevenueCat, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability provided by the GDPR: write to the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with its update date.
